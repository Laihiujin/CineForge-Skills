# Governance

CineForge Skills is maintainer-led and developed in public. The maintainer sets project direction, reviews changes, manages releases, and may delegate ownership as the contributor base grows.

Decisions prioritize reproducibility, production safety, vendor neutrality, explicit state management, and practical filmmaking value. Significant changes should begin with an issue or discussion and include migration notes when they alter schemas, manifests, or skill contracts.

The project is continuously iterated and optimized. Backward compatibility is preferred, but pre-1.0 structures may evolve when a clearer production contract materially improves the library.
