# Continuity Taxonomy

- Identity: facial geometry, age, body, hair, marks.
- Wardrobe: garment, closure, wear, dirt, damage, accessories.
- Props: owner, hand, orientation, fill level, damage, location.
- Spatial: screen direction, axis, eyelines, subject positions, entrances/exits.
- Temporal: time, weather, wetness, injuries, destruction, light progression.
- Performance: emotional value, breath, tension, pace, gaze, interpersonal distance.
- Visual: palette, exposure, contrast, grain, focal family, camera behavior.
- Audio: room tone, acoustic space, perspective, ambient continuity, dialogue energy.
