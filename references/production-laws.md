# Production Laws

## Canon lock
A stable visual fact is written once and referenced by ID. Do not restate it differently from shot to shot.

## State delta
Each shot has `INHERIT`, `CHANGE`, and `MUST_NOT_CHANGE`. The next shot inherits the resolved end state.

## Reference hierarchy
Identity > wardrobe/prop > environment > composition > style. When two references conflict, the higher-ranked reference wins unless the shot explicitly overrides it.

## Spatial continuity
Track screen side, facing direction, eyeline target, subject depth, contact points, entrances/exits, camera side of axis, and geography.

## Temporal continuity
Track time-of-day, weather, wetness, damage, dirt, blood/injury, prop state, food/liquid level, light sources, and any irreversible action.

## Generation economics
Do not spend generation attempts on unresolved story, design, continuity, or reference conflicts. First make the shot *earnable*.
