# Cinematography Grammar

Describe camera intent in physical terms: shot size, subject distance, lens behavior/FOV, camera height, angle, movement path, speed, stabilization, parallax, depth-of-field intent, focus behavior, shutter/motion character, exposure intent, and transition. Avoid meaningless stacks of camera-brand names.

Coverage should preserve editability: establish geography, protect eyelines, secure reaction shots, vary shot size purposefully, and avoid crossing the axis without motivation or a neutral bridge.
